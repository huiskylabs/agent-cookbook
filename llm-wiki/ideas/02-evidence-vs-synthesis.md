# Evidence Vs Synthesis

## Universal Idea

Separate evidence from synthesis.

Evidence is what the source says or shows. Synthesis is the interpretation built from evidence.

Mixing them makes the system harder to audit, trust, and update.

```text
evidence -> synthesis -> future use
```

## Why It Matters

Evidence and synthesis have different jobs.

Evidence preserves traceability. Synthesis makes knowledge usable.

If they collapse into one layer, readers cannot tell what came from a source, what was inferred, and what changed over time.
